About the Creators

Paul and Sara Brinnel met while living in Chicago in 2017. They got married in 2020 in Colorado and now live in New England with their rescue pup, Archibald. Paul and Sara love playing board games, especially deck builders like Dominion, Dune: Imperium Uprising, Clank! Catacombs, and Slay the Spire. They started working on "Cards for Cowboys" in 2025. They've been having a blast collaborating and competing as the game continues to evolve.
