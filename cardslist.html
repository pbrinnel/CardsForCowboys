Cards List

Every card in the game — the 10-card starter deck plus all 54 Store cards. Rows are Acts, columns are the three suits. Tap any card to see it full size, and see Reading the Cards for what the symbols mean.

Each Store card is unique at 2–4 players. At 5–8 players you shuffle two copies of each Act deck together.
