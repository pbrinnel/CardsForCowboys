Rules

Objective

A push-your-luck deck builder. Each round you choose how hard to push your luck drawing from your own deck — the deeper you go, the more $ and Cows you turn up, and the closer you get to three Bandits and losing the round.

Your goal: build the biggest Herd of Cows.

Play rounds until the Store runs out, then everyone lays out their whole deck for The Showdown and counts their Cows. 2–8 players.

Setup

  1. Each player takes an identical 10-card starter deck — 4 River, 4 Rattlesnake, 2 Cactus. Shuffle it face-down as your draw pile.
  2. Split the 54 Store cards into three Act decks by the cowboy hats in the bottom-right corner (18 per Act). Shuffle each Act deck separately.
  3. Build the Store once, face-down, back to front: Act 3 at the back, Act 2 in the middle, Act 1 at the front. Each Act is 2 rows (2–4 players) or 3 rows (5–8 players) — see the table for row width.
  4. Stagger every other row by half a card, brick-wall style, so two cards cover the one above. Flip the front row face-up.

Sequence of Play

1. Draw

  • Everyone draws at the same time, one card at a time off your draw pile. You must draw at least one; after that you may stop whenever you like.
  • Run out? Shuffle your discard pile into a new draw pile and cut one card from top to bottom. If both piles are empty, stop drawing for the round.

Bust. The moment you have 3 Bandits in front of you, you bust: score nothing, no buy, and everything you drew goes to your discard pile.

2. Buy

  • If you didn't bust, add the Cows you drew to your Herd. Busted players sit this phase out.
  • On your turn you must buy or burn one card — no passing. Buy any face-up Store card you can afford with the $ you drew (cost is in the bottom-left corner); it and everything you drew go to your discard pile. Or burn a face-up card out of the game.
  • Then discard your drawn cards and start a new round.

Always reveal. As soon as a card leaves the Store, turn face-up anything behind it that is no longer covered.

Who goes first

  1. Most $ this round — that player chooses who buys first.
  2. Tied? Most Cows this round.
  3. Still tied? Most cards drawn.
  4. Still tied? Compare drawn-card cost by position — 1st vs 1st, 2nd vs 2nd. First higher cost wins.
  5. Still tied? Random draw.

Play continues clockwise from whoever was chosen, skipping busted players. Same rule every round, including the first.

The last card ends the round. When the final Store card is bought or burned, the round stops. Anyone yet to take a turn misses it — but if you didn't bust, you still score the Cows you drew.

3. The Showdown

Everyone — including anyone who just busted — lays out their whole deck face-up and adds every Cow they own to their Herd. Most Cows wins.

Example. Jo has 14 Cows banked from scoring rounds. She lays out her deck and counts 9 more printed across her cards, for 23. Cows on cards she never drew still count.

If Herds are tied

  1. Most $ printed across your deck.
  2. Still tied? Most cards in your deck.

Anyone still level shares the win.

Reading the Cards

Suits

River — safe. Never has Bandits.
Cactus — up to 1 Bandit.
Rattlesnake — 1–2 Bandits, best rewards.

The suit shows on the back as well as the front, so you always know the risk of the card you're about to draw.

Symbols

$ — money to spend this round.

Cow — goes to your Herd. Cows are how you win.

Bandit — your 3rd in a round busts you.

Double Bandit — counts as 2.

Jail — cancels 1 Bandit.

Explosive — one-time use, see above.

Fine Print

  • You may check the card backs in your draw pile. You may not look through your discard pile.
  • Burn always means removing a Store card from the game. To consume one of your own cards you use an Explosive.
  • Deck = every card you own: draw pile, drawn cards and discard pile.
  • Draw 4s stack. If a mandatory draw is itself a Draw 4, it counts as one of the draws you owed and adds 4 more on top — owe 4, draw a Draw 4, now owe 7. Busting cancels any draws still owed.
  • Hidden Herd (variant) — keep Herd totals secret until the Showdown.

Quick Reference

Each round

  1. Draw together. 3 Bandits = bust.
  2. Score your Cows, unless you busted.
  3. Buy or burn one card each — no passing.
  4. Reveal uncovered cards, discard, repeat.

Ties

Buy order: most $ → most Cows → most cards drawn → card cost by position → random.

Final Herd: most $ in deck → most cards in deck → share the win.
