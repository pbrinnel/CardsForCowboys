Rules

Objective

Cards For Cowboys is a push-your-luck deck builder. You're building a cattle herd out on the frontier, and every round you choose how hard to push your luck drawing from your own deck — the deeper you go, the more $ and Cows you turn up, and the closer you get to three Bandits and losing the round.

Your goal: build the biggest Herd of Cows.

How a round works

  1. Draw as many cards off your draw pile as you dare. Draw a 3rd Bandit and you bust — you score nothing this round.
  2. Score the Cows you drew into your Herd, if you didn't bust.
  3. Buy one card from the Store with the $ you drew and add it to your deck — or burn one out of the game if nothing's worth buying.

Then everyone discards, and a new round starts. Repeat until the Store is empty.

How the game ends

The game ends the round the last card leaves the Store. Everyone then lays out every card they own for The Showdown and totals up their Cows. The biggest Herd wins.

2–8 players.

Setup

What you need

  • 54 Store cards — 18 for each Act, marked by the cowboy hats in the bottom-right corner. 5–8 players use two copies of each Act.
  • 10 starter cards per player — 4 River, 4 Rattlesnake, 2 Cactus. Every player's starting deck is identical.

Setting up

  1. Take your own 10-card starter deck. Shuffle it and keep it face-down as your draw pile.
  2. Separate the Store cards into three Act decks by the cowboy hat number in the bottom-right corner (Act 1 / 2 / 3). Shuffle each Act deck on its own.
  3. Build the Store once, at the start of the game — there is no setup between Acts. Deal it face-down in three bands, back to front: Act 3 at the back, Act 2 in the middle, Act 1 at the front. Each band is 2 rows (2–4 players) or 3 rows (5–8 players). Use the table below for the row width at your player count.
  4. Stagger every other row by half a card, brick-wall style, so two cards cover the one above. Flip the front row face-up to start.

Sequence of Play

1. Draw Phase

  • Draw cards one at a time from the top of your draw pile. Everyone draws at the same time — you don't wait for each other.
  • You must draw at least one card. After that you may stop whenever you like.
  • If your draw pile runs out and you want to keep drawing, shuffle your discard pile to make a new draw pile, then cut one card from top to bottom.
  • Some cards are Explosives you can use during this phase — see Symbols on Cards. A few cards have extra timing rules in Card Clarifications.

Bust. The moment you have 3 Bandits in front of you, you bust. Your Draw Phase ends, you score nothing this round, you don't buy or burn, and everything you drew goes to your discard pile.

2. Buy Phase

  • If you didn't bust, add the Cows you drew this round to your Herd. If you busted, you skip the rest of this phase — you don't buy or burn.
  • On your turn you must either buy or burn one card. You cannot pass.
  • Buy one card from the Store, spending the total $ you drew this round. Every Store card shows its cost in the bottom-left corner; you can buy any face-up card whose cost you can cover. Put the card you bought, and everything you drew, into your discard pile.
  • Or burn one card — take a face-up Store card out of the game — if you can't afford anything or don't want what's on offer.

Always reveal. As soon as a card leaves the Store, turn face-up any card behind it that is no longer covered. It's easy to forget, and it's what puts the next cards on offer.

Buy Order

  1. The player with the most $ this round chooses who buys first.
  2. If tied: most Cows this round.
  3. If still tied: most cards drawn.
  4. If still tied: compare the cost of your drawn cards one position at a time — 1st card against 1st card, then 2nd against 2nd, and so on. The first position where one player's cost is higher decides it.
  5. If still tied: a random draw decides.

Whoever is chosen buys first. Play then continues clockwise from them, skipping anyone who busted. The same rule picks the first player every round, including the first.

  • Once everyone still in the round has bought or burned, put your drawn cards in your discard pile and start a new round.

The last card ends the round. When the final card is bought or burned out of the Store, the round stops there. Anyone who hasn't had a turn doesn't get one — but if you didn't bust, you still score the Cows you drew.

3. The Showdown

When the last card leaves the Store, the game does not end straight away. Every player faces The Showdown first.

  • Lay out your whole deck face-up — draw pile, drawn cards and discard pile together.
  • Count every Cow you own and add them all to your Herd.
  • Everyone does this, including anyone who busted in the final round.

The player with the most Cows in their Herd wins!

Example

Jo ends the game with 14 Cows in her Herd from scoring rounds. She lays out her deck and counts 9 Cows printed across her cards — 5 on one Rattlesnake, 2 on another, and 1 each on two more. Her final total is 23. Cows on cards she never drew still count; everything you own is counted once.

If Herds Are Tied

  1. Most $ printed across your whole deck.
  2. If still tied: most cards in your deck.

Anyone still level after that shares the win.

Card Backs & Suits

Every card belongs to one of three suits, visible on both the front and back. The suit tells you roughly how risky the card is to draw.

River — Safe; never has Bandits.
Cactus — Moderate risk; may contain 1 Bandit.
Rattlesnake — High risk, high reward; may contain 1–2 Bandits.

Symbols on Cards

Reading a card

Three things always sit in the same place, so you can read any card at a glance:

  • Top-right: the suit — River, Cactus or Rattlesnake.
  • Bottom-left: the cost — what you pay to buy the card from the Store. This is a plain $ number in the corner.
  • Bottom-right: the Act — cowboy hats, one per Act (Act 1 / 2 / 3). You use these when you sort the Store into its three decks during Setup.

Everything in the middle of the card is what it does when you draw it:

$ — Money you gain when you draw the card, to spend in the buy phase. It sits in a circle in the middle of the card — don't confuse it with the plain $ in the bottom-left corner, which is the card's cost, not money you gain.

Cow — Add the number of Cows shown to your Herd (if you don't bust).

Bandit — Caution! Draw your 3rd Bandit and bust immediately.

Double Bandit — Counts as 2 Bandits. Caution! Draw your 3rd Bandit and bust immediately.

Jail — Negates 1 Bandit.

Explosive — A one-time-use card. It does nothing when drawn; on your turn you may use it — its effect applies immediately and the card is then permanently removed from your deck. (Note: using an Explosive removes it from your deck; this is different from burning, which removes a card from the Store — see Errata.)

Errata

  • You may freely check the card backs in your draw pile.
  • You may not look through your discard pile.
  • Burn = Remove a Store card from the game without buying it (returned to the game box). "Burn" always refers to a Store card — to consume one of your own cards, see Explosive above ("use").
  • Draw = take the top card of your draw pile.
  • Deck = every card you own — draw pile, drawn cards and discard pile together.
  • If both your draw pile and discard pile are empty, stop drawing for the round.

Card Clarifications

  • Draw 4s stack. If one of your mandatory draws is itself a "Draw 4", it counts as one of the draws you owed and then adds 4 more on top of what's left — it doesn't replace them. Example: you owe 4 mandatory draws. Your first one turns up another Draw 4: that clears one of the four, leaving 3, then adds 4 more, so you now owe 7. There's no limit to how many times this can chain.
  • Mandatory draws you still owe are cancelled if you bust.

Strategy Tips

  • Tracking which cards you haven't drawn gives you a big edge. Start by remembering whether you've drawn your double-Bandit card yet.
  • Balance the risk of busting against the reward of gaining more $ and Cows.
  • It's not always best to draw as many cards as possible. If you have enough $ for this round, consider stopping to set up a strong hand next round.

5+ Player Rules

Plays up to 8 — same rules, only setup changes:

  • Shuffle in a second copy of each Act deck to fill the larger Store.
  • The Store is 9 rows (3 per Act) instead of 6 — see the Setup table for the row width at each player count.

Quick Reference

The whole game on one screen, for when you already know how to play.

Each round

  1. Draw — everyone at once, one card at a time. At least one card, then stop when you like. 3 Bandits = bust (score nothing, no buy).
  2. Score — if you didn't bust, your Cows go to your Herd.
  3. Buy or burn — one card each, no passing. Clockwise from the first player, skipping busted players.
  4. Reveal anything no longer covered, discard, and go again.

Buy order ties

  1. Most $ this round — that player picks who buys first
  2. Most Cows this round
  3. Most cards drawn
  4. Cost of drawn cards, 1st vs 1st, 2nd vs 2nd…
  5. Random draw

Game end

The last card leaving the Store ends the round. Then everyone lays out their whole deck and adds up every Cow they own. Most Cows wins.

Herd ties: most $ in your deck → most cards in your deck → share the win.

Easy to forget

  • You must buy or burn — you can't pass.
  • Reveal uncovered cards every time one leaves the Store.
  • You may check the backs in your draw pile, but never look through your discard pile.
  • A Draw 4 drawn during forced draws stacks.

Variants

  • Hidden Herd — Opponents' herd totals stay secret until the final showdown.
